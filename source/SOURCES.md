# Sources and evidence rules

## Community guide collection supplied by the owner

Original research text: `geo guide.txt`, uploaded to this conversation, *not redistributed inside this package*. This edition paraphrases and reorganises general information. Named contributors:

- **Balee_92**: *Interactive dependency map of all 107 goods* (recorded around 10 Nov 2026). Its recipe matrix and hosted interactive dataset were not included in the supplied text. Goods counts and critical-input counts are historical observations, not an up-to-date database.
- **warmaninger**: *Beginner's guide: your first year, and what to do when things go wrong* (noted game 1.1.0, build 26519199; updated 9 Nov 2026). The author differentiates code inspection, calculations, developer statements and observations from a single older campaign. This guide preserves that caution.
- **grayassembly**: *Geoplanetical: 10 Things I Wish I Knew Before Playing* (2 Nov 2026). General beginner experience, not authority for exact mechanics.
- **Traslo**: Early-launch inflation guide (updated 1 Nov 2026). Historical view of interest rates; superseded as universal advice by the fact that inflation was reworked in later updates.

## Developer release notes

- **Official Steam community news:** https://steamcommunity.com/app/940230/allnews/
- **1.1.4 patch mirror, originally supplied by the game developer:** https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/26708962/

**Do not treat patch notes as proof of game behaviour beyond what they expressly state.** A feature in an announcement is developer-confirmed; exact balance thresholds or effects need current-build testing. The 1.1.4 patch announcement originally accompanies build 26708962, with later public build 26709373 on the same day; do not misstate the announcement's own build ID.

## Evidence labels

- **Developer-confirmed:** Patch notes expressly announce the change, but not necessarily its quantitative effectiveness.
- **Community observation:** A contributor describes or tested it. Version may differ.
- **Community code reading:** A contributor inferred it from code and may not have tested in gameplay.
- **Editorial example:** An invented scenario illustrating a decision. Not a report of a real campaign.
- **Unverified after patch:** The guide deliberately does not claim that the old formula remains identical.
